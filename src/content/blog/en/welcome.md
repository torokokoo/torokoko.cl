The blog will be a lightweight place for writing about backend work, cybersecurity, tools, experiments, and anything worth keeping around. No heroic publishing schedule promised; an honest log is more useful than a perfect machine.

If you found your way here, hi. You can reach me through LinkedIn, GitHub, or by email.
